Technical report: actual assemblies, methods and source data

Amazing Hand

Initial pilot complete · no ranked entries. The retained case uses 29 component designs in 226 positions. Three Astra attempts and three supervised Grok Bot attempts establish a baseline under the stated execution and scoring conditions. No complete reference-pose reconstruction was obtained; these observations do not establish a model ranking.

ORCA

In preparation · no ranked entries. Repeated import and rigid-placement checks passed for five flagged source components. Their known geometry defects remain documented exceptions, not successful validity checks. Source-version and parts-list conflicts remain unresolved.

How results will be compared

Each published comparison must identify the hand, kit and scoring versions, model and effort, runtime, assistance conditions, and attempted and graded run counts. Repeated trials must report failures and variation, not just the best export.

Hand results are not comparable with M3-CRETE scores. Initial preparation concerns digital assembly placement; it does not establish grasping performance, strength, safe motion or physical buildability. Berkeley Lite is deferred.

Next steps

Retain the Amazing Hand task and scoring for future model-update comparisons. ORCA still needs a matching authored assembly or accepted component placements before its baseline can proceed. The broader campaign remains incomplete; Berkeley Lite can be added later.
