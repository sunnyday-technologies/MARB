How results will be compared

Each published comparison must identify the hand, kit and scoring versions, model and effort, runtime, assistance conditions, and attempted and graded run counts. Repeated trials must report failures and variation, not just the best export.

Hand results are not comparable with M3-CRETE scores. Initial preparation concerns digital assembly placement; it does not establish grasping performance, strength, safe motion or physical buildability. Berkeley Lite is deferred.

Before scoring begins

Reconcile the source components and reference assembly; freeze separate worker kits and held-out references; validate hand-specific scoring; then qualify the runtime and automated execution. No evaluation campaign has started.
